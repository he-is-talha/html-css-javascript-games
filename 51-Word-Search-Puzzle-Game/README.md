# Word Search Puzzle Game 🔤

A classic, interactive word search puzzle game built entirely with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript.

## Features

- **4 Diverse Categories**: Web Development, Tech & Computing, Animals, and Space & Astronomy.
- **Dynamic Procedural Generation**: Words are randomly placed horizontally, vertically, and diagonally in any direction with automatic non-conflicting letter placement.
- **Mouse & Touch Support**: Intuitive drag-to-select mechanics on both desktop mice and mobile touch screens.
- **Built-in Sound Synthesizer**: Uses the native HTML5 Web Audio API for sound effects (letter selection, word match, win celebration) without requiring any external audio files.
- **Hints System**: Highlights the starting letter of an unfound word when stuck.
- **Live Stats**: Tracks elapsed time, live score with speed bonuses, and found word count.
- **Self-contained**: Zero external build dependencies or libraries.

## How to Play

1. Choose a category from the dropdown menu.
2. Search the 10×10 letter grid for words displayed in the "Words to Find" sidebar.
3. Click and drag across adjacent letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
4. When a correct word is formed, it will highlight in green and cross off your word list.
5. Find all 10 words to solve the puzzle!

## Controls

- **Desktop**: Click and drag with your mouse across letters.
- **Mobile / Tablet**: Touch and drag your finger across the grid.
- **Hint**: Click the "Hint" button to illuminate a starting letter.
- **New Game**: Click "New Game" to generate a fresh grid anytime.
